The Metro Manila Subway alignment follows surveyed rights-of-way and subsurface easements: Mindanao Avenue Extension (Ugong Depot), Quirino Highway, Mindanao Avenue median, VMMC Golf Course curve, Vertis North into Centris, Heroes St/BIR Rd/East Ave, Katipunan Avenue, White Plains subterranean curve, Meralco Avenue, Pasig River crossing, Bonifacio Global City 11th Avenue, Lawton Avenue, and bifurcates into the NAIA T3 spur and NSCR Bicutan mainline.
